# Code of Conduct

Hava81 is committed to a constructive and respectful project environment.

When participating in issues, pull requests, reviews or other project spaces:

- discuss ideas and code without personal attacks,
- give specific, actionable feedback,
- assume good intent while remaining clear about technical concerns,
- avoid harassment, discriminatory language, threats or deliberate disruption,
- respect privacy and do not publish personal or sensitive information.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.

If a conduct concern involves sensitive information, contact the maintainer privately rather than posting the details publicly.
